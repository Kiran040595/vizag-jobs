## Before you sign up

# Eligibility requirements for AdSense

[Previous: Make sure your site's pages are ready for AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563?hl=en&ref_topic=1319756)[Next: Owning the site you want to use to participate in AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/91205?hl=en&ref_topic=1319756)

If you have your [own content](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724#content) that [meets our policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724#policies) and [you're 18 or over](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724#age), you can sign up for AdSense. [#signup](https://support.google.com/adsense/search?q=signup)

To participate in the AdSense Program, you need to meet our eligibility requirements. Here are some things to check before you sign up for an AdSense account.

Check out the [AdSense site approvals video series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLbAFD4oU9Ycr4j1pViNjkS82rhbF293H8) for more information.

## Do you have your own unique and interesting content?

Your content must be high-quality, original, and attract an audience. Read our tips to [make sure your site's pages are ready for AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563).

**Note**: It's important that you can access the HTML source code of the site you submit for AdSense. Learn more about [owning the site you want to use to participate in AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/91205).

## Does your content comply with the AdSense Program policies?

Make sure that your site complies with our [Program policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182) before you sign up. Keep in mind that we may change our policies at any time, and per our [Terms and Conditions](https://adsense.google.com/adsense/terms), it's your responsibility to keep up-to-date with them.

## Are you at least 18 years old?

As noted in our [Terms and Conditions](https://adsense.google.com/adsense/terms), we can only accept applications from applicants who are at least 18 years of age.

**Note**: If you're under 18, you may have a parent or guardian sign up for AdSense using their own Google Account. If their AdSense account is approved, all payments will be made to the adult responsible for the site.

[Sign up for AdSense](https://adsense.google.com/adsense/signup?utm_source=internal&utm_medium=et&utm_campaign=adsense-help-acq&utm_term=https://goo.gl/eAyfUu&utm_content=HC-ART-ID-1631343)

## If you use Blogger (or another host partner) or YouTube

If you use an AdSense [host partner](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2534778) (like [Blogger](http://blogger.com/)), you can sign up for a hosted AdSense account and, if you are a YouTube creator, you can sign up for an AdSense for YouTube account. Note that to be eligible, you must meet certain eligibility requirements. When your blog or channel becomes eligible for monetization via AdSense or AdSense for YouTube, as applicable, you can set up your account and link these products.

Please sign up for AdSense via your Blogger or host partner account, and for AdSense for YouTube via YouTube Studio. For more information, visit:


- [Advertise on your Blogger blog](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/50288)
- [Set up an AdSense for YouTube account to get paid on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/troubleshooter/7367438)

To monetize with Blogger, you must comply with the AdSense [policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10502938) and [restrictions](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10437795).
